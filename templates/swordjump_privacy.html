Sword Jump - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the mobile game "Sword Jump" ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you play.

1. Who operates this app

Sword Jump is developed and operated by an independent developer. Contact: nikandagames@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

If you choose to sign in with Google (optional, required only to use the global leaderboard):

  • Your Google account name
  • Your Google account email address
  • Your Google account profile picture URL

This is provided to us by Google's Sign-In service (via Firebase Authentication) when you explicitly choose to sign in. If you never sign in, none of this is collected.

Gameplay data:

  • Your best score, coins, and unlocked characters/weapons - stored locally on your device only, not sent anywhere unless noted below.
  • If signed in and you finish a run, your score and display name are sent to our database (Firebase Firestore) to power the optional global leaderboard. Leaderboard entries (display name and score) are visible to other players.

3. How we use this information

  • To let you sign in and identify your account across sessions
  • To show your ranking on the global leaderboard (weekly, monthly, all-time)
  • To prevent obviously implausible scores from appearing on the leaderboard

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing.

4. Third-party services

The App uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database) to provide sign-in and the leaderboard. Google's own privacy policy governs how Firebase infrastructure handles data in transit and at rest: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay data stays on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Leaderboard entries and account data linked to your Google sign-in are kept until you request deletion. To request deletion of your data, email nikandagames@gmail.com and we will remove your leaderboard entries and associated account data.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Security

Data sent to our servers is transmitted using standard encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) via Google Firebase's infrastructure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: nikandagames@gmail.com
